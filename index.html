1. The Three Core Considerations

According to the National Association for the Education of Young Children (NAEYC), teaching decisions must balance three essential pillars:

1. Commonality Universal age-related developmental milestones and cognitive progression shared by all human children.
2. Individuality Each child's unique developmental pace, personality, learning style, and neurodiversity.
3. Sociocultural Context Familial customs, community traditions, and native language (Mother Tongue) that give meaning to children's experiences.

2. Combating the "Push-Down" Curriculum

A major hazard in early childhood education is the push-down curriculum—forcing young children to sit for hours doing repetitive, abstract paper-and-pencil worksheets. DAP advocates for purposeful play, hands-on sensory exploration, and collaborative problem-solving.
